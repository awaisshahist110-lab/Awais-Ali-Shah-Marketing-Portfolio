05

Lead generation

My two favourite strategies.

Useful content should create attention, build familiarity and make the next conversation warmer.

01

Starting without an audience

Build with the people your market already trusts.

  1. 01
    Find the credible voicesPreferably before someone calls them thought leaders.
  2. 02
    Ask about the hair-on-fire problemThe question their audience already wants answered.
  3. 03
    Turn the answers into something usefulA report, lead magnet or series worth opening.
  4. 04
    Give it a route into the marketCredible contributors, relevant networks, no desperate repost requests.

Result: original insight, borrowed credibility and warmer conversations.

02

Starting with existing customers

Your customers are probably sitting on the campaign.

  1. 01
    Talk to the best customersBefore their story disappears into a CRM note.
  2. 02
    Find the repeated problemEspecially the one you solved unusually well.
  3. 03
    Build the proof into a seriesCase studies, campaign angles and sales-ready stories.
  4. 04
    Show it to buyers with the same problemThey recognise themselves before the sales call begins.

Result: content built from proof and leads that arrive warmer.
