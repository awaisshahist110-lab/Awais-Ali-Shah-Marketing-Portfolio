What I actually do

Strategy is cheap. I ship the thing.

Most companies do not need another deck telling them to be customer obsessed. They need someone who will interview the buyers, write the positioning, build the content engine, run the ads, chase the handoff when leads go cold, and report honestly on what is not working.

That is the job I take. If there is a CMO in place, I execute the whole plan. If there is not, I build the foundation and help hire the senior person once the revenue justifies it.

I came into marketing from an engineering degree. It shows in how I work: I would rather instrument a funnel and find the real leak than run a fifth round of creative testing on a page nobody is reaching.
