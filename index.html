Lead generation

My two favourite strategies.

Starting without an audience

Build with the people your market already trusts.

  1. 01

    Find the credible voices

    Preferably before someone calls them thought leaders.

  2. 02

    Ask about the hair-on-fire problem

    The question their audience already wants answered.

  3. 03

    Turn the answers into something useful

    A report, lead magnet or series worth opening.

  4. 04

    Give it a route into the market

    Credible contributors, relevant networks, no desperate repost requests.

Result: original insight, borrowed credibility and warmer conversations.

Starting with existing customers

Your customers are probably sitting on the campaign.

  1. 01

    Talk to the best customers

    Before their story disappears into a CRM note.

  2. 02

    Find the repeated problem

    Especially the one you solved unusually well.

  3. 03

    Build the proof into a series

    Case studies, campaign angles and sales-ready stories.

  4. 04

    Show it to buyers with the same problem

    They recognise themselves before the sales call begins.

Result: content built from proof and leads that arrive warmer.
