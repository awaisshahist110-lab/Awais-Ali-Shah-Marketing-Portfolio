Xgrid · March 2023 to December 2024

A pipeline built from 100+ interviews with C-Suite Leaders

I joined Xgrid as a copywriter and later moved into marketing strategy. Instead of writing at the market, I interviewed it: 123 C-suite leaders working across marketing automation, SaaS and enterprise technology.

Those conversations became executive research reports, landing pages, case studies, ad creative, partnership decks, webinars and a 300-article SEO programme. The interviews also helped warm the exact accounts the sales team wanted to reach.

The method

The research was not preparation for lead generation. It was the lead generation.

By the time a report was published, the people quoted in it already knew who we were. Their insights gave the content credibility, gave sales warmer conversations and supplied material for search, social, email and outbound.

Tinova · April 2026 to present

Selling the outcome, not the deck.

When I joined Tinova, it had no functioning content pipeline, its offer was associated mainly with pitch decks, and the CEO's LinkedIn engagement came largely from people outside the company's ICP. Today, the company page is currently reaching founders, investors and industry voices beyond its follower base. Client campaigns · B2C

Homecare, retirement and family protection.

Campaign and funnel work for US homecare providers, independent financial advisers and insurance agencies. Projects covered homecare enquiries, retirement-income planning, life insurance policies, tax-efficient wealth planning and family protection.

Across the homecare accounts, the recurring problem was not always lead volume. Enquiries were often being lost through slow callbacks, unnecessary landing-page steps and mismatched calls to action.

I helped shorten the journey from ad to conversation while developing creative around the moments that trigger a homecare decision: caregiver exhaustion, fear of another fall and the promise to keep a parent at home.

For financial and insurance campaigns, I produced audience research, campaign angles, landing pages and ad copy that translated unfamiliar products into recognisable financial decisions.
